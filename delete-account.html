Delete Account - Foviox

How to Delete Your Account

1 Open the Foviox app
2 Go to your Profile tab
3 Tap the Settings (gear icon)
4 Tap "Security"
5 Scroll down and tap "Delete Account"
6 Type "DELETE" to confirm
7 Tap "Delete Account" button

What Data Will Be Deleted

  • All your posts, photos, and videos
  • All likes and comments
  • Your followers and who you follow
  • Saved posts
  • Personal information (email, username, profile picture)

What Data Will Be Retained

  • No personal data will be retained

Data Deletion Timeline

All data is deleted immediately. Backups are removed within 30 days.
